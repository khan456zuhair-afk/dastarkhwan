## Brand & Style

This design system translates the time-honored heritage of subcontinental royal hospitality into a modern digital platform. Inspired by the ceremonial grandeur of traditional communal banquets, the aesthetic bridges Mughal architectural geometry with contemporary editorial luxury. 

The emotional tone evokes evening warmth, intimate low-lit dining rooms, polished brass cookware, and fragrant spice profiles. It targets discerning gourmands who seek authentic heritage cuisine delivered with Michelin-grade modern presentation. 

The visual strategy merges **tactile luxury** with **modern dark minimalism**:
- **Background Atmosphere:** Deep, warm near-blacks and aged espresso tones replace clinical cold slates, providing an intimate visual sanctuary.
- **Opulent Metallic Accents:** Restrained brushed golds and warm saffron highlights denote hierarchy and action without visual clutter.
- **Architectural Rhythm:** Generous spacing, structured hairline dividers reminiscent of filigree jaali screens, and subtle warm-toned ambient glows create tangible depth.

## Colors

The palette embraces the warmth of charcoal braziers and gilded banquet vessels, calibrated exclusively for dark mode to preserve richness and appetite appeal.

### Canvas & Surface Hierarchy
- **Base Canvas (`#0D0D0F` to `#141416`):** The foundational night canvas, absorbing distractions and drawing full attention to food photography.
- **Secondary Surface (`#1E1A17` to `#26211C`):** Rich espresso tones used for containers, persistent bars, and navigation panels.
- **Elevated Surfaces (`#221E1B` to `#2A2521`):** Subtle tonal lift for actionable cards, modal dialogs, and floating sheets.

### Accents & Metallics
- **Primary Accent (`#D4AF37` / `#E5C158`):** Muted opulent warm gold. Reserved for critical calls to action, selected states, royal badges, and focal typography highlights.
- **Secondary Accent (`#E07A24` / `#B85D19`):** Deep saffron and charred amber. Applied to order progression indicators, spice heat scales, and limited chef specials.
- **Status Tones:** Functional states are grounded in botanical and mineral pigments:
  - *Success / Vegetarian / Halal Certification:* Emerald Olive (`#4E8752`)
  - *Attention / Wait Times:* Saffron Amber (`#D9822B`)
  - *Destructive / Error:* Kashmiri Ruby (`#C2413A`)

### Typography Colors & Borders
- **Text Primary (`#FAF7F2`):** Antique cream with warm undertones to eliminate screen harshness.
- **Text Secondary (`#B8AFA6`):** Oat flour stone for descriptions, dietary labels, and timestamps.
- **Text Muted (`#8C827A`):** Low-contrast tertiary metadata.
- **Borders & Dividers:** Fine hairline dividers configured as `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.16)` on highlighted components or `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` on generic structural grid bounds.

## Typography

Typography establishes an intentional contrast between classic editorial warmth and programmatic legibility.

- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Conveys the historical prestige of dastarkhwan dining halls. Used for dish titles, collection headers, and section narratives. High-contrast transitional serifs provide grace without losing punch on dark canvases.
- **Body & Controls (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Ensures clear legibility across complex culinary descriptions, customization option groups, allergen warnings, and checkout workflows.
- **Tracked Editorial Labels (`label-caps`):** Rendered in uppercase with generous character tracking (`0.14em`) for category anchors, origin markers (e.g., *AWADH*, *LAHORE*, *PESHAWAR*), and nutritional badges.
- **Price Treatment:** Numbers are paired with `Playfair Display` or medium-weight sans to read like a bespoke dining menu rather than a utilitarian receipt.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model balances structured discipline with airy negative space, avoiding the cluttered density typical of fast-food delivery interfaces.

### Grid Framework
- **Desktop (12 Columns):** Fluid container capped at 1380px maximum width. Margin defaults to `3.5rem` with a `2rem` gutter (`gutter-lg`). Menu layouts utilize asymmetrical 4/8 column distributions (navigation & category anchors alongside culinary feed).
- **Tablet (8 Columns):** Fluid scaling with `2rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (4 Columns):** Single column stream with sticky horizontal category filters. Margins set to `1.25rem` with `1rem` gutters (`gutter-sm`).

### Rhythm & Alignment
Spacing follows an 8-point compositional scale with a 4-point micro-step:
- Compact groupings (spice badges, ingredient pills, price tags) rely on `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px).
- Card interiors and modular field groupings employ `space-md` (16px) to `space-lg` (24px).
- Section transitions and royal course banners breathe via `space-xl` (40px) and `space-2xl` (64px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy on dark surfaces avoids sharp drop shadows, relying instead on warm ambient diffusion, tonal stacking, and luminous hairline borders.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Canvas (Level 0):** Hex `#0D0D0F`. Pure background; anchors full-bleed visual assets.
2. **Structural Panels (Level 1):** Hex `#1A1613`. Sticky headers, cart drawers, and category rails. Layered with an ultra-fine border: `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.05)`.
3. **Actionable Dish Cards (Level 2):** Hex `#221E1B`. Floating on the canvas with an ambient shadow: `0 12px 32px -8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.65), 0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)`. Enhanced with an inner golden perimeter highlight: `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.14)`.
4. **Modals & Overlays (Level 3):** Frosted glass panels using `background: rgba(26, 22, 19, 0.82)` paired with `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` and illuminated by a localized top rim light: `0 -1px 0 0 rgba(229, 193, 88, 0.25) inset`.

### Warm Glow Accents
Primary active states (such as active cart indicators or hero order triggers) feature a warm ember diffuse glow:
- `box-shadow: 0 4px 24px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.22), 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.

## Shapes

The shape philosophy is refined and architectural, utilizing soft geometric corner treatments (`roundedness: 1` — 4px base radius) rather than aggressive circles or sharp brutalist edges.

- **Base Components (Inputs, Buttons, Cards):** Standardized on `0.25rem` (4px) to `0.5rem` (8px) outer corner radii. This preserves the structured elegance of formal table layouts.
- **Signature Dish Imagery:** Image frames maintain an `8px` corner radius, occasionally framed within an asymmetrical arch motif (`border-radius: 120px 120px 8px 8px`) for feature editorial banners and signature dishes (e.g., Dum Pukht Biryani).
- **Capsule Badges (Dietary & Spice Indicators):** Micro-chips and round tags use full pill geometry (`9999px`) to visually contrast against the structured rectilinearity of menu cards.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Imperial CTA:** Solid gold gradient background (`linear-gradient(135deg, #E5C158 0%, #D4AF37 50%, #B38E22 100%)`), text color `#0D0D0F` (heavy contrast), bold sans typography, subtle 4px rounded radius. Hover initiates a gentle lift and gold glow diffusion.
- **Secondary / Ghost Button:** Transparent background, `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`, cream text (`#FAF7F2`). On hover, the surface shifts to `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.08)` with border color shifting to `#D4AF37`.
- **Destructive Action:** Subtle slate tone with crimson edge `rgba(194, 65, 58, 0.4)` and cream text.

### Dish & Menu Cards
- **Architecture:** Contained inside elevated espresso `#221E1B` containers with a `1px` stroke of `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`.
- **Arrangement:** High-resolution 4:3 ratio imagery topped with pill metadata (Spice Grade, Portion Size). Below the media, the dish name is set in `headline-sm` (`Playfair Display`), followed by secondary description and an interactive bottom bar integrating the tracked price and a compact gold `+ Add` counter trigger.

### Spice-Meter Indicators
- Custom segmented visual scale consisting of 3 to 4 micro flame/chili pip dots.
- Dormant pips use `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.15)`; active pips fill with Saffron Amber (`#E07A24`) or Kashmiri Ruby (`#C2413A`) with a subtle `2px` drop blur.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- Compact capsules (`roundedness: 3`, pill-shaped).
- **Halal / Vegetarian:** Subdued dark olive backdrop (`rgba(78, 135, 82, 0.15)`) with emerald typography (`#75B37A`) and a subtle green dot icon.
- **Chef's Reserve / Signature:** Warm gold border with `rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.1)` fill and `#FAF7F2` text.

### Form Inputs & Customization Fields
- Dark inset backgrounds (`#141416`), border `1px solid rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.1)`.
- Focus shifts border to `#D4AF37` with an ambient glow of `0 0 0 3px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.15)`.
- Selection handles (Checkboxes & Radios) use gold fills with `#0D0D0F` custom check checkmarks and concentric inner rings.

### Tab & Category Navigation
- Horizontal sticky scroll bars on secondary dark brown espresso (`#1E1A17`).
- Active category items do not use heavy filled blocks; they utilize a pristine cream font with a hairline brushed-gold underline indicator and subtle top atmospheric illumination.
